Our Process

01 — Understand

We learn the role, compensation, schedule, requirements, and what success looks like.

02 — Source

We actively search for people who match the position rather than waiting for applicants.

03 — Interview

We conduct an initial interview and assess qualifications, communication, motivation, and fit.

04 — Present

You receive the candidate's resume plus our recruiter assessment and relevant notes.

05 — Coordinate

We stay involved through employer interviews, communication, and next steps.

06 — Place

When the right candidate is hired, we stay connected through the placement process.
